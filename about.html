Pointing back to the people who did the work

The Point of View curates independent Indian journalism. We pull reporting worth your attention into one place, summarise it plainly, and always link back to the journalists and outlets who did the work.

Every post credits its sources. If you're a journalist and want a correction or a different credit, get in touch through our Instagram.
